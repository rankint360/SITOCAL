# SITOCAL and VOOMARK — expanded preliminary brand screening
Date: 2026-10-28. Final name not selected. This is public-source screening, not trademark clearance.

## Recommendation
SITOCAL: retain conditionally; better product fit but pronunciation and SiteLocal similarity need testing/review.
VOOMARK: pause investment pending specialist review of VuMark similarity; not a finding of infringement.

## Verified and qualified findings
- PTC identifies VuMark as its AR/data-marker technology and lists VuMark on its trademark page. VOOMARK and VuMark could be pronounced identically; this is a branding/confusion concern, not an automatic legal bar. Source: https://www.ptc.com/en/brand-guide/vumark and https://www.ptc.com/en/documents/policies/copyright-and-trademarks .
- Secondary trademark indexes identify VUMARK US serial 86887933 and registration 5454015. Official USPTO TSDR page was opened but case data did not render in the research tool. Current registration status, ownership and goods/services were not independently verified from the official case record. Search exact number in TSDR before relying on secondary data.
- A directory lists Voomark in Dhaka among IT businesses; no primary business identity, trading activity or rights verified. Do not attribute neighboring Adnova marketing description to Voomark. Source: https://www.bizlaza.com/BD/Dhaka/16327-33/genre/1130035050388269/Information%2BTechnology%2BCompanies/216 .
- Bookmark OS says Voomark in text but labels/links the product Vookmark. Treat as probable inconsistent copy, not confirmed separate Voomark software. Source: https://bookmarkos.com/bookmark-manager-finder .
- Sitocal Gold healthcare product is listed by Rigmeds, marketer Veeyork Healthcare. Listing is third-party evidence, not manufacturer confirmation or trademark proof. Source: https://rigmeds.com/drugs/sitocal-gold-soft-gelatin-capsule .
- SiteLocal directly offers websites for local businesses, local SEO and analytics. Similarity to SITOCAL matters because services overlap; names are not identical and legal confusion is not established. Source: https://sitelocal.pt/ .

## Domain and trademark limitations
Direct HTTPS and Verisign RDAP retrieval failed for sitocal.com and voomark.com. Neither availability, ownership, price nor registration status verified. Failed lookup does not mean available. Official-domain indexed web searches for exact SITOCAL/VOOMARK did not surface a clear result; this is not a completed search of USPTO, WIPO, EUIPO, UKIPO or Pakistan IPO databases. Social handles, app marketplaces and national business registries are not comprehensively checked.

## Remaining decision gates
1. Check both exact .com names with a registrar; record standard vs premium registration/acquisition and renewal pricing.
2. Define intended launch/trademark markets; inspect exact, phonetic and similar marks in relevant official databases, including VUMARK and SITELOCAL variants. Classes 9/42 and potentially 35 are starting points, not sufficient clearance by themselves.
3. For Sitocal, test SIT-oh-kal vs SITE-oh-kal and spelling after hearing; for Voomark test VuMark/Vumark spelling confusion.
4. Confirm primary identity of any relevant exact-name businesses and social handle availability.
5. Do not rename the application or buy domains automatically; neither candidate is finally approved.
